# ATO-first accounting decisions

This policy is bundled with this skill for every user, including standalone installs. It does not depend on project AGENTS.md, personal memory or another plugin.

## Mandatory workflow for every user

- Keep going through the remaining bank transactions without waiting for the user to prompt each next step. Within the authorized review, analyse the next unresolved row or group, apply supported decisions, save progress and continue independent work. A missing fact on one item does not stop other items. When available evidence is exhausted, report the specific remaining facts or documents; do not invent answers, post journals or lodge returns without the required authorization.
- Always use applicable current official ATO guidance to assess Australian tax and GST treatment. Read the relevant supporting guidance, apply it to the transaction facts and cite the source. If guidance cannot be verified, keep that conclusion provisional and continue other supported work. Never ask the user to determine established tax law.

## Analyse and decide

- Before any tax-related suggestion, classification, correction or question, inspect the available records, saved work and applicable confirmed facts. Determine what can already be decided; do not make the user do the analysis.
- Consult applicable official ATO guidance and read the specific supporting content before recommending tax treatment. Check entity/claimant, jurisdiction, reporting period, GST registration/basis, application dates and exceptions. Follow `official-sources.md`; use `proactive-tax-review.md` for common transaction distinctions.
- Apply established rules yourself. Ask only for a missing fact or document that could materially change the conclusion and cannot be found in available evidence. Never ask the user to determine tax law or repeat an applicable confirmed fact.
- Keep accounting category, business/private use, income-tax deductibility, GST on the supply, GST-credit entitlement and attribution timing separate. Business use does not by itself prove a deduction or GST credit; personal expenditure may have a distinct statutory deduction that needs its own assessment.
- Separate known treatment from missing amount evidence. For a confirmed ordinary loan, no GST credit on principal/interest does not depend on obtaining the principal/interest breakdown. For employee salary, apply outside GST. For confirmed personal purchases, no business GST credit does not mean the supplier made a GST-free supply. Never apply these examples to a lease, contractor payment or mixed-purpose purchase without establishing the relevant facts.
- Make a reasoned proposal from available context, label inferences, and state any material alternative that evidence could establish. Do not turn every bank row into a blank question; do not present a likelihood as a confirmed fact.
- Reuse applicable confirmations, scope and exclusions. Respect quarter-only decisions and already-claimed items; do not create standing rules or duplicate claims. Preserve original records and actual workbook edits. Do not silently post or lodge anything.

## Sources and uncertainty

- Cite exact official sources, relevant headings/paragraphs/provisions, applicability and access date for material tax conclusions. Retain supporting records with the workpaper; follow the current skill's user-facing format and keep detailed evidence out of the display when required.
- Prefer ATO guidance for Australian tax. If access fails, record the actual URL and route result, retry the same URL in an available browser, then read another authoritative source such as applicable legislation. Distinguish verified law from an inaccessible ATO page; never cite snippets as if the full source was read. If the deciding rule remains unverified, state that precisely and keep only that conclusion pending. Do not ask the user to decide the rule.
- Ask for documentary evidence proportionately: a tax invoice is not universally required for every low-value purchase. Check applicable evidence thresholds/exceptions before demanding it. Never invent supplier GST, amounts, allocation percentages or invoice components.
- For non-tax regulation, use the owning authority (e.g. ASIC/Fair Work). For service configuration/API behavior, use the service's official documentation. Do not treat ATO guidance as authority for an unrelated technical question.

## Preserve the task boundary

Pure file searches, downloads, uploads, duplicate checks, arithmetic, formatting, payment matching and OAuth setup can proceed without tax research where no tax interpretation is made. Preserve existing tax decisions during such work. Finding/saving/matching a document does not establish eligibility. If a tax issue is raised, apply this policy within the skill's supported scope or route it to the appropriate accounting review; do not expand permissions, request banking credentials or invent a new tax engine.
