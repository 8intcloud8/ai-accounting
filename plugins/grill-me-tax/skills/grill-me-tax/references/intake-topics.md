# Question coverage

Use these as screening topics, not one long questionnaire. Derive follow-up questions from the facts and current year-specific ATO instructions.

## Shared facts and records
- Entity/claimant, legal structure, activities, period, original/amended return, start/stop dates, relevant residency and ownership changes.
- All bank, credit-card, cash, merchant/platform and foreign-currency channels; statement coverage and opening/closing balances.
- Sales/income sources, invoices/RCTIs, refunds, grants, insurance/compensation, asset sales, barter and overseas transactions.
- Expense records, business purpose, private use, asset ownership, who incurred/paid the expense, reimbursements and claims already made elsewhere.
- Prior lodged returns/BAS, assessments, accountant adjustments, opening balances, carried-forward items and outstanding corrections.

## BAS branch
- GST registration dates/changes, reporting frequency, cash/non-cash basis, actual reporting method and labels shown on the activity statement.
- Sales types and locations, exports/imports, GST-free/input-taxed activities, special arrangements such as GST groups or margin scheme where applicable.
- Invoice/payment dates, unpaid/part-paid balances, credit notes, bad debts and changes in use.
- Capital purchases/disposals, mixed-purpose costs and GST supporting evidence, including import documentation.
- Employees/director wages, withholding, PAYG instalment notices, fringe benefits instalments, fuel use and any other obligations shown on the statement. Screen specialist taxes when activities trigger them.
- Previously reported amounts, corrections and annual adjustments. Do not substitute a GST-only workpaper for all applicable BAS labels.

## Annual business branch
- Financial statements, debtors/creditors, stock, work in progress, prepayments/accruals and accounting/tax timing differences.
- Assets acquired/disposed of, dates first used/ready for use, finance/leases, depreciation schedules and business-use evidence.
- Payroll, super payments and timing, contractors, benefits, reimbursements, entertainment, donations and other items needing tax adjustments.
- Services mainly generated by an individual's efforts, contracts, client concentration, payment terms, equipment, rectification liability, other workers and premises. These facts support a PSI/PSB assessment.
- Loans, interest, related parties, overseas dealings, capital gains/losses, prior tax losses, ownership changes and relevant elections.
- Company follow-ups: director/shareholder payments and loans, personal benefits, dividends/franking, group relationships and turnover/passive-income evidence relevant to concessions.
- Partnership/trust follow-ups: governing agreements/deeds, partners/beneficiaries, allocation/distribution records and dates, unpaid entitlements, elections and prior schedules. Route complex determinations to specialist review.

## Individual branch
- Residency and changes, employment and government payments, interest, dividends, managed funds, trust/partnership distributions, business/gig income and foreign income/tax.
- Rental properties, ownership shares, availability/private use, finance, repairs/improvements and acquisition/disposal records.
- Property, shares, crypto and other asset disposals/transfers with cost-base and prior loss records.
- Work expenses, vehicles/travel, home working, education, donations, tax-agent costs, reimbursements and evidence.
- Personal super contributions, acknowledgement records, reportable contributions, income tests and education-loan obligations where relevant.
- Spouse/relationship dates and relevant income, dependants, private hospital cover and Medicare entitlement/exemptions where relevant to the return.
- Reconcile prefill with actual statements and missing income. Ask age/date-of-birth facts only where a rule requires them.
