# Official-source lookup

## When to consult

Before recommending, confirming or changing tax/regulatory treatment, read this reference and open relevant official guidance using the host's available browsing/search tools. Pure document extraction, discovery, saving, arithmetic reconciliation and payment matching can proceed without browsing when no rule is being interpreted. A match or extracted GST amount does not establish tax eligibility. These references do not expand this skill's supported calculations or approval authority.

## Verification workflow

1. Establish the entity type, jurisdiction, relevant income year/reporting period, registration/basis and the issue to decide. Reuse confirmed context; ask only for facts that change applicability.
2. Select the relevant topic below. Search the appropriate official domain for a specific rule, then open the specific page or ruling, following relevant official links. These are starting points, not an exhaustive knowledge base. Discover other official guidance when the topic requires it.
3. Read the actual content supporting the decision. Check effective/application dates, the period covered, exceptions, entity eligibility and any withdrawn/superseded or draft status. Current pages may not apply to a historical period. Distinguish law/rulings from general explanatory material; resolve conflicts or leave the issue unresolved.
4. Cite the exact official page beside each material tax/regulatory conclusion. Include the relevant heading, ruling paragraph or provision where available, why it applies and the date accessed in YYYY-MM-DD. Separate supplied facts and calculations from sourced rules and inferences. Prefer applicable official guidance over local course notes or remembered rules.
5. Retain a concise source record with the draft workpaper under the selected workspace's output/YYYY-MM-DD/ using a new filename. Use the user's local date. Record issue, entity/period, title, exact URL, section/paragraph, application/effective dates where stated, access date, status and conclusion/limitation. Do not invent an effective date or imply access proves legal completeness. Reuse an existing source record for the same issue/period in the current task unless new facts or a currency concern require another check.

## Access failures

If browsing is unavailable, or a page returns 403/404, times out, requires login or contains no usable guidance, record the attempted URL/date and actual failure. Use a focused official-domain search to locate a moved page or another official source; open and read it before citing it. Search snippets and a successful HTTP response alone are not verification. Do not bypass access controls or transmit client records to public search: use generic rule/entity/period terms, without personal identifiers, invoice contents or account information.

For any conclusion still dependent on unavailable guidance, state 'Official guidance not verified', identify the unresolved rule and keep the affected tax treatment pending. Continue unaffected extraction/calculations and clearly labelled drafts; do not turn missing verification into a zero GST amount or an approved claim. Previously saved sources may be cited with their actual date and stale/unverified status, never as freshly checked. Do not overwrite a good saved source with an error page or silently substitute an unofficial article.

## Host and scope

The plugin supplies instructions and source links; the host must supply browsing/search access. Do not claim the package installs a browsing tool or automatically refreshes sources in the background. Preserve existing user-confirmation, accounting evidence and scope requirements. Payroll/super and company/workplace links support issue identification or applicable review only; they do not add a payroll engine or a complete tax/BAS calculation.
