# ATO guidance

Researched 12 October 2026. Recheck relevant guidance for the period before finalisation. These are paraphrases, not universal allocation percentages.

## Actual costs and usage records

[ATO actual-cost method](https://smallbusiness.taxsuperandyou.gov.au/claiming-deductions-for-your-home-based-business/actual-cost-method): retain expense evidence and the basis for business/private allocation. Its home-business guidance describes a representative four-week usage diary each financial year, excluding non-working periods. Appliance electricity is illustrated using operating time and attributable power costs. This does not authorise estimating every household expense from floor area alone.

[ATO calculating running expenses](https://smallbusiness.taxsuperandyou.gov.au/claiming-deductions-for-your-home-based-business/calculating-running-expenses): evidence and exclusion of ordinary private costs matter. Select the method appropriate to the taxpayer and space.

## Company reimbursement

[ATO company and trust expenses](https://smallbusiness.taxsuperandyou.gov.au/claiming-deductions-for-your-home-based-business/company-and-trust-expenses): a documented direct reimbursement of the business portion of home running costs is distinguished from rent/occupancy payments. The business claims the reimbursed running expense; the homeowner does not make the same deduction in the personal return. Sole-director status does not remove this distinction.

## GST

[ATO claiming GST credits](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/claiming-gst-credits): establish registration, eligible business acquisition, GST in price and supporting tax-invoice requirements. Credit entitlement and income-tax deduction treatment are separate. Check attribution for the confirmed GST basis.

[ATO GSTR 2001/3, employee reimbursements](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=GST/GSTR20013/NAT/ATO/00001): reimbursement-related GST credits have specific conditions. Holding an employee's qualifying tax invoice can meet documentation requirements; an unsupported weekly estimate is not reimbursement evidence.

[ATO working-from-home guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/deductions-you-can-claim/working-from-home-expenses): compare actual-cost and applicable fixed-rate methods only for an eligible taxpayer. Refresh current-year rates. Do not use an income-tax cents-per-hour rate to calculate a BAS GST credit or separately deduct costs already covered by that rate.
